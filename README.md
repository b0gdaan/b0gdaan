### Research in context

My master's research studies **rolling correlations**, not direct price forecasts.
The reported results favor simple persistence-based models: Ridge, AR(1) and HAR
are close in RMSE, while the last-value baseline has the best average MAE.
These results do not establish a profitable trading strategy.
